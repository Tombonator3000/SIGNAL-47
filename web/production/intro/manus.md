# Frosset intromanus

Fem originale engelske avsnitt. SIGNAL / 47 er visbar merkevare og har ikke et eget lydklipp.

1. New Mexico, 1986. Twenty-seven antennas listen to a sky that has never answered. On the night shift, silence is a measurement. It can be filed, signed, and forgotten.

2. Tonight, the instruments will record something the sky has not sent. A telephone will ring in an empty room. A photograph will remember what no one saw.

3. Your name is Reyes. Your shift ends at dawn. Between now and then, forty-seven seconds will refuse to belong to any clock.

4. The record calls it interference.

5. The record is incomplete.
