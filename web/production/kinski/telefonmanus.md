# Telefonmanus: Kinski synthetic parody

24 eksisterende talereplikker, med original engelsk ordlyd og aktørlabels. Labels og sitattegn leses ikke inn. De samme tekstene brukes i spillets telefonbank og de ferdige lydfilene. Ingen nye replikker er skrevet.

## Kapittel 2: Ward, kontrollrommets telefon

**WARD:** That is enough to hold the morning series. It is not enough to tell us why it happened.

**WARD:** Check the reference record. If the instrument inherited the fault, the record may tell us from where.

## Kapittel 2: Nora, nøkkelholderen på Sierra Motor Court

**N. VEGA:** Sierra Motor Court.

*Stille spilleraside, ingen stemme: You give your name and SARO, and read her the survey number from the sleeve.*

**N. VEGA:** Station One. Nobody has asked me for that key in a long time.

**N. VEGA:** Don't send me copies. Go out to the station first and look at the cable, where it was cut. Then bring me the originals.

## Kapittel 3: Nora, felttelefonen på STATION 01

**N. VEGA:** So you went out there.

**N. VEGA:** This line still rings in the motel office. It has since 1947. Nobody ever took it down.

**N. VEGA:** You have seen the cable now. It did not break by itself.

**N. VEGA:** Room 6. Bring the original record and your film, developed. Then I will tell you the rest.

## Kapittel 4: rom 6 og den gamle feltlinjen

**YOU:** Where does that phone go?

**N. VEGA:** To the office. At night the office line is patched through to this room, and the other end of it is the field hut. It has been since 1947.

**N. VEGA:** You left the receiver on out there. Listen.

**N. VEGA:** Tomás. Where did you leave the spare key?

**THE LINE:** Reference west. No. East. Hold the last reading.

**N. VEGA:** That is what he said then. Not an answer to what I asked now.

## Kapittel 5: Ward, telefonautomaten på dineren

**E. WARD:** Ward.

**YOU:** It is Reyes. I am at the diner on the old road.

**E. WARD:** I read the telex. All of it.

**E. WARD:** Hondo called. So did Site 11.

**YOU:** What did they say?

**E. WARD:** Enough. I am not doing this on a pay phone.

**YOU:** I am checking the old road first.

**E. WARD:** Then check it, and come back. I want you on the console before the morning series.

**E. WARD:** Six o'clock, Reyes.

Kilde: `src/core/phoneScripts.ts`, basert på de eksisterende scenene i `src/story/Chapter2.ts` til `Chapter5.ts`. Ni nye lydklipp er laget for kapittel 2 og 3; de øvrige 15 gjenbrukes fra den første leveransen. Dette er en syntetisk parodistemme.
